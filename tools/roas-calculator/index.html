Kristian's Method

The reasoning behind the numbers above.

Revenue ÷ Ad Spend = ROAS

The formula

ROAS is the ratio between revenue and ad spend. A 2× ROAS means $2 back for every $1 spent. Simple enough — but revenue isn't profit.

VAT COGS Ship Fees Ad budget

What gets eaten first

Every sale carries costs that chip into revenue before ad spend gets a slice: VAT, product cost, shipping, payment fees. What's left over is your real ad budget.
