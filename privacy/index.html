Privacy Policy

How your data is collected, used and protected when you use this site or sign up to the newsletter.

Last updated: May 2026

Who this applies to

This site (kristianjennings.com) is run by Kristian Jennings ("I", "me", "my"). This policy explains what personal data is collected when you visit the site, use the free tools and resources, or subscribe to the email newsletter, and what your rights are. If you have any questions, contact me at hello@kristianjennings.com.

What I collect

  • Email address — only if you choose to subscribe to the newsletter. Under GDPR, the legal basis here is your consent — you give it when you submit the form, and you can withdraw it any time by unsubscribing.
  • Technical & usage data — when you visit, my hosting provider automatically logs standard information such as your IP address, browser type, device, referring page and the pages you view. This is used to keep the site secure and running, not to identify you personally. Under GDPR, the legal basis is legitimate interests — keeping the site secure, available and free from abuse.
  • Nothing from the tools — the calculators, generators and libraries on this site run entirely in your browser. Anything you type into them (for example, figures in the ROAS Calculator or text in the iMessage Screenshot Maker) stays on your device and is never sent to or stored by me.

The email newsletter

If you subscribe, I collect your email address so I can send you the newsletter. The newsletter is managed through Kit (formerly ConvertKit), which stores and processes your email on my behalf as my email service provider.

  • Subscriptions use double opt-in — after signing up you'll get a confirmation email, and you're only added once you click the link inside.
  • I use your email solely to send the newsletter and occasional related updates. I never sell or rent it.
  • Kit may record standard engagement data such as whether emails are opened or links are clicked, to help me understand what's useful.
  • You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of every email. Once you unsubscribe, you'll stop receiving emails.

You can read how Kit handles data in their privacy policy.

Cookies

This site uses very few cookies. Essential cookies and similar technologies may be set by the newsletter form and by my hosting provider to keep the site working and secure. The newsletter form (provided by Kit) may set cookies to handle your signup correctly. I do not use advertising cookies or sell data to advertisers. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as intended.

Third-party services

To run this site I rely on a small number of trusted providers who process data on my behalf:

  • Kit — email newsletter delivery and subscriber management.
  • Cloudflare — website hosting and content delivery, which involves processing technical data such as IP addresses to serve pages and protect against abuse.

Each provider handles your data under their own privacy terms.

International data transfers

Both Kit and Cloudflare are based in the United States, which means your data may be processed outside the EU/EEA. These transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards — specifically Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission under Article 46 of the GDPR — so your data stays protected to EU standards even when it leaves the region.

How long I keep your data

I keep your email address for as long as you stay subscribed to the newsletter. If you unsubscribe, your details may be retained by Kit in a suppressed/unsubscribed state so you aren't accidentally re-added, or removed on request. Server logs held by my hosting provider are kept only for a limited period for security and operational purposes.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data I hold about you.
  • Ask me to correct or delete it.
  • Withdraw consent (for example, by unsubscribing).
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these, email me at hello@kristianjennings.com and I'll respond as soon as I reasonably can.

If you're in the EU and feel I haven't handled your data properly, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In France, that's the CNIL — you can reach them at cnil.fr.

California residents

If you live in California, the CCPA gives you a few extra rights on top of the ones above. The short version: I do not sell your personal information — not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone. There's no opt-out needed because there's nothing to opt out of. You still have the right to know what data I hold about you, ask for a copy, and ask me to delete it. Same email as above and I'll take care of it.

Children

This site and newsletter are not directed at children, and I don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided their data, contact me and I'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. When I do, I'll change the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be made clear.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email me at hello@kristianjennings.com.
